Active matchmaking

Active matchmaking is for people who want a partner in the process of finding theirs. I’ll get to know you, offer honest perspective and coaching as you date, and actively search for potential matches. I’ll screen each possibility with care before making an introduction. This can be especially helpful if you’d value steady guidance or don’t have the time to do all the searching yourself. Active matchmaking is currently available to clients in California, with a focus on the Bay Area and Los Angeles.

Your active matchmaking package begins with an included 90-minute dating consultation. Monthly support includes one 50-minute coaching session, complimentary pre-date prep, regular updates, and brief post-date feedback.
