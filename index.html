Who belongs here

If you identify as Jewish and are looking for, or open to, a Jewish partner, you belong here. You don’t need to express your Jewish identity in any particular way.

Jewish singles of all ages are welcome here. I offer active matchmaking to clients in California, with a focus on the Bay Area and Los Angeles. My free database is open to Jewish singles anywhere and draws on an international network, so I highly recommend joining. I meet people from all over!
