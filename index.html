Who belongs here

If you identify as Jewish and are looking for, or open to, a Jewish partner, you belong here. You don’t need to express your Jewish identity in any particular way.

My hands-on matchmaking services are currently focused on California, especially the Bay Area and Los Angeles. Wherever you live, you’re welcome to join my free database. My network extends internationally, and I’ll reach out if a potential match comes to mind.
