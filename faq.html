FAQ

Do I need to be Jewish?

For now, Meet Through Danit is focused on Jewish matchmaking. If you identify as Jewish and are looking for, or open to, a Jewish partner, you’re welcome here. This focus may expand in the future.

Where do you currently work with clients?

I currently offer active matchmaking to clients in California, with a focus on the Bay Area and Los Angeles, and specialize in working with people ages 20 to 50. Dating consultations are available wherever you live, and my free database is open to Jewish singles of all ages. I’ll reach out if a possible match comes to mind. I’m also happy to connect you with another professional matchmaker when that makes sense.

Do I need to be religious?

No. If you identify as Jewish and are seeking or open to a Jewish partner, you’re welcome here.

What’s the difference between the database, a dating consultation, and active matchmaking?

The free database lets me consider your profile for introductions. A dating consultation gives us time to talk through your dating life and get to know you beyond the form. If I meet someone who seems like a good fit, I’ll reach out about making an introduction. Active matchmaking includes dedicated time searching for potential matches and supporting you through the process.

Can I do coaching without matchmaking?

Yes. You can book a 50-minute coaching session for guidance on a specific dating question, without matchmaking or a prior consultation. The optional 90-minute consultation is a broader conversation about your dating life and also helps me get to know you with possible introductions in mind. A 30-minute pre-date call is available on its own.

Will you ask for feedback after dates?

After an introduction, I’ll check in to hear how the date felt for you and, when possible, ask for the other person’s perspective.

How many introductions can I expect?

I care more about the quality of an introduction than a quota of dates. A potential match depends on mutual interest, fit, and timing, so I don’t promise a set number or outcome. I’ll be clear about the search and support included in the option you choose.
