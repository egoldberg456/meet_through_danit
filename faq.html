FAQ

Do I need to be religious?

No. If you identify as Jewish and are seeking or open to a Jewish partner, you’re welcome here.

What’s the difference between the database and active matchmaking?

The free database lets me keep your profile in mind when a potential match comes up. Active matchmaking includes dedicated time getting to know you, looking for possibilities, and supporting you through the process.

Can I do coaching without matchmaking?

Yes. Gold is a one-time conversation about your dating experiences. If you’d like more guidance afterward, you can book individual coaching sessions or a four-session package.

Will you ask for feedback after dates?

When possible, I’ll listen to how the date felt for you and may ask the other person for their perspective.

How many introductions can I expect?

I care more about the quality of an introduction than a quota of dates. A potential match depends on mutual interest, fit, and timing, so I don’t promise a set number or outcome. I’ll be clear about the search and support included in the option you choose.

Where do you work?

I’m initially focused on California in general, with an emphasis in the Bay Area and Los Angeles. You can still reach out from elsewhere, and I’ll be candid about how I may be able to help.
