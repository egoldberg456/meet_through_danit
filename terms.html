Terms

Last updated September 28, 2026. Joining the database or writing to me means you’re comfortable with the following.

The service

Meet Through Danit offers a free database, dating coaching, and active matchmaking for people who identify as Jewish and are looking for, or open to, a Jewish partner. You don’t need to express your Jewish identity in any particular way.

Who it’s for

This is for adults 18 and older. I’m initially focused on California, with an emphasis on the Bay Area and Los Angeles. You can still reach out from elsewhere, and I’ll be candid about how I may be able to help.

The database

Joining the database is free. It lets me keep your profile in mind when a potential introduction comes up. It is not a promise of a date, a match, or a particular timeline.

Paid support

Gold, Platinum, and Diamond are separate from the free database. Current rates are listed on the Services page. Diamond is custom, and we’ll agree on the scope together before you commit. Paying for support still depends on mutual interest, fit, and timing.

No set number of introductions

I care more about the quality of an introduction than a quota of dates. I don’t promise a set number or a particular outcome. I’ll be clear about the search and support included in the option you choose.

What you share

Please send information that is true, and only share someone else’s details if you have a right to. Be respectful in messages and, if we make an introduction, with the other person. I may decide not to move forward if it isn’t a fit.

The site

The words and design of this website belong to Meet Through Danit. These terms can be updated as the practice grows. How your information is handled is described in the privacy policy.
