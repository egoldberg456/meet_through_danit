Terms & Conditions

Last updated November 19, 2026. These terms explain how the free singles database and Meet Through Danit services work.

1. Eligibility and the free database

Meet Through Danit offers a free singles database, dating consultations, coaching, and active matchmaking. The database and services are for adults 18 and older. The current matchmaking focus is people who identify as Jewish and are looking for, or open to, a Jewish partner.

Joining the database is free and lets me keep your profile in mind for possible introductions. You can decline any introduction or ask me to pause or remove your profile.

2. Paid services

The Offerings page describes current services. Before you commit to paid support, review the service terms provided for your booking or agreement, including the price, scope, and applicable cancellation, rescheduling, and refund terms. For ongoing matchmaking, review the written agreement for billing and duration as well.

These website terms describe the general service. The terms for the specific booking or written agreement govern your purchase, subject to your rights under applicable law.

3. Introductions and outcomes

I’ll describe the search and support included in your service. Potential introductions depend on mutual interest, fit, and timing. Joining the database or purchasing support doesn’t guarantee an introduction, a date, a relationship, or a particular timeline.

Dating coaching offers personal guidance about dating and communication. If you need mental health care or crisis support, seek help from a qualified professional.

4. Privacy and respectful conduct

Share accurate information about yourself and update me when it changes. Please respect other people’s boundaries and privacy. Keep profiles, screenshots, photos, and contact details shared for an introduction private unless the other person gives permission to share them.

Harassment, threats, impersonation, and repeated unwanted contact can lead to a pause or removal from the database. See the Privacy Policy for how I handle your information.

5. Dating safety and reporting concerns

Take time to decide whether you feel comfortable meeting someone. For an early date, consider meeting in public, arranging your own transportation, and letting a friend know where you’ll be. An introduction is never a guarantee of someone’s conduct or safety.

If you experience harassment, unwanted contact, or a safety concern connected with an introduction, email danit@meetthroughdanit.com. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

6. Website use and content

Use the site lawfully and respect the privacy of others. Please don’t scrape personal information, impersonate anyone, or interfere with the site. You’re welcome to share links to public pages. Ask before reusing Meet Through Danit’s original writing, photos, or branding, except where the law permits reuse.

7. Contact preferences and third-party services

You can choose whether to receive calls or texts about your profile or potential introductions. To change your preferences, email danit@meetthroughdanit.com. Newsletter signup is a separate choice. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Intake and booking links may take you to services such as Airtable and Cal.com. Those services have their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Questions and updates

If you have a question about these terms or a booking, email danit@meetthroughdanit.com. I’ll post changes to these website terms here with the revision date at the top of the page.
