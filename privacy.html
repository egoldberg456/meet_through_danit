Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026. Here’s how I handle what you share with Meet Through Danit.

Information I collect

When you join the database, you may share your name, contact details, birth date, location, photos, Jewish identity, dating preferences, and other information about yourself. Some answers can be sensitive, including details about your religion, who you’re interested in dating, or your political preferences. Share only what you’re comfortable providing.

If you book a call, email me, or work with me, I may receive your scheduling and correspondence details, service and payment records, feedback, and notes from our conversations. If I ask to use a recording or transcription tool for a call, I’ll explain it and ask for your permission first.

How I use your information

I use it to manage your profile, consider you for introductions, contact you about possible matches or your profile, and provide and administer services you request. I may contact you to confirm details, arrange a session, or follow up after an introduction. You can tell me how you prefer to be contacted.

Newsletter signup is optional. You can unsubscribe from newsletters while continuing to receive messages about your profile or services.

What I share and with whom

Profiles are private and are not posted publicly. I may share limited profile details relevant to a potential introduction so each person can decide whether they’re interested. I’ll ask before sharing your direct contact information with a potential match or sending your profile to another matchmaker. I don’t sell your personal information.

Service providers may process information to help run the practice. These include Airtable for intake and client records, Cal.com for scheduling, and providers for email, document storage, website hosting, and payments. I may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to address a safety or security issue.

Website and third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google and links to separate services, including Airtable and Cal.com. The website host and third-party services may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and pages or resources requested. When you follow a link, the destination may use cookies or similar technologies under its own privacy policy.

I don’t use this website to track your activity across other websites or change its behavior in response to browser Do Not Track signals. Third-party services may handle these signals differently.

Retention and your choices

I keep profiles while they are in the database and may keep service and communication records as needed to operate the practice, meet legal or accounting obligations, or resolve disputes.

Email danit@meetthroughdanit.com to ask to review, correct, or delete your information, pause introductions, or change how I contact you. I may check that the request came from you. Some records may need to be retained for legal or operational reasons.

Adults and policy updates

The database and services are for adults 18 and older. If you believe a minor has submitted information, please contact me so I can review it.

If this policy changes, I’ll post the revised version here and update the effective date. I’ll notify affected clients of material changes by email when I have their contact information.
