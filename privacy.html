Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated September 28, 2026. This is how I handle what you share with Meet Through Danit.

What you share

The profile form asks for your name, email, phone if you offer it, city, age, a bit about you, who you’re hoping to meet, and which kinds of support interest you. It also asks you to confirm that you identify as Jewish and are looking for or open to a Jewish partner, and that you are 18 or older.

The question form asks for your name, email, the topic, and your question.

How I use it

I use your profile to keep you in mind for a potential introduction, and to send the introductory email about Meet Through Danit and the ways we can work together. I use questions to reply to you. I use either form to follow up if you ask about coaching or matchmaking.

Introductions

I don’t post profiles on this website, and I don’t sell your information. If I think two people may be a thoughtful match, I may share relevant parts of what you told me so each of you can decide whether you’d like to meet.

How long I keep it

I keep your profile while you’re in the database, and I keep questions long enough to respond and follow through. You can ask me to update or delete what I have.

Asking for a change

Send a note through the question form and I’ll take care of updates or removal. I’m based in California, with a focus on the Bay Area and Los Angeles.
